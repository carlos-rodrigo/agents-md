# New Surface or Approved Redesign

Use this only when no coherent visual authority exists or the user approved replacing it. A missing design-system file does not make an established product greenfield; inspect the rendered product and code.

## Discover

Ask only what changes the result:

- Who arrives, from where, and what must they accomplish or understand?
- What proof, content, assets, and product mechanism are real?
- What must remain recognizable or untouched?
- Which mode—Operate, Read, Persuade, Experience—owns the surface?
- What would make a polished result feel wrong?

Do not ask users to select CSS values or choose from canned style genres.

## Ground visual direction

Find material in the subject's real world:

- tools, notation, artifacts, environments, and rituals;
- existing brand assets and language;
- publications, interfaces, and visual systems the audience recognizes;
- the product's unique mechanism or interaction.

Generate 3 materially different structural concepts from the content and task. Compare them on task clarity, audience recognition, system reach, asset feasibility, accessibility, and performance. Present options only when a real product/visual decision remains; otherwise choose and explain the strongest direction.

## Direction contract

Before implementation, state:

```text
Thesis:
Surface mode:
Audience scene and job:
First viewport / initial state:
Information path:
Visual system: type, color roles, spacing, shape, imagery
Product-specific signature:
Primary action/result:
Critical states:
Risk and constraint:
```

A signature is optional. It must embody the subject or product mechanism—not merely be unusual. Keep surrounding UI quiet enough for hierarchy and task completion.

## Build

- Author representative content and states; label illustrative values.
- Establish semantic tokens before scattered values.
- Define component anatomy and interaction states.
- Keep one visual grammar across navigation, content, controls, and feedback.
- For Persuade, demonstrate the mechanism or proof instead of repeating claims.
- For Operate, expression never obscures task, state, or familiar controls.
- For Read, comprehension and retrieval outrank decorative atmosphere.
- For Experience, ensure controls, reduced motion, and fallback remain available.

A redesign replaces visual language but preserves approved product truth, content, function, and constraints. Do not smuggle a redesign into a feature request.
